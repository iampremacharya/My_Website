Glimpse at It...

Hanged, Ranged & Estranged in the walls for years & years. Hardly ever been gazed and stared at But, When I did, That frame worthed much more, than that I could ever have dreamt One thing fascinating about it !! You stare, talk, quarrel or even fight, what you get in return is just 'Quiet' Up there for forever, being mere need to be taken cared as it seems to be very rare ...
