01 / Biography

Engineering, Research & Building

I am Prem Acharya, a Computer Engineering student at Institute of Engineering, Thapathali Campus, Tribhuvan University. I am interested in understanding how computing, engineering, mathematics, and the physical world can be connected to solve practical problems.

My work spans software development, artificial intelligence, engineering systems, computational methods, and independent research. I enjoy turning ideas into working prototypes, experimenting with unfamiliar technologies, and documenting what I learn along the way.

Beyond coursework, I actively participate in student-led research, technical communities, and engineering initiatives. My broader goal is to develop the ability to move from a problem or research question to a technically sound solution.
